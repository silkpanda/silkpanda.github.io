Privacy Policy

This app accesses only the Google Tasks data of the Google account that authorizes it. That data is used solely to read and manage that user's tasks.

OAuth tokens and task data are stored only on the user's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by this app, and no data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
